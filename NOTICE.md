## AndroidLiquidGlass (Kyant0)
Liquid-glass surfaces (backdrop blur, vibrancy, lens refraction, highlight, shadow) use
`io.github.kyant0:backdrop:1.0.6` and `io.github.kyant0:shapes:1.2.0`.
